## Decision and purpose

Prepare one capture of missing **training-source response maps**, from the
unchanged cosine-nine research control, before specifying a new recognition fit.
Use all 26 existing training blocks/3,329 frames. Retain the maps and extract the
same native-plus-strict-.01 candidate pool without label-dependent selection.
The new product is reusable training-source evidence, not another tracker.

The intended learning question is whether a basketball-specific, point-conditioned
image model can assess a candidate against explicit rejection, while preserving
weak/partial balls. That is a direction to investigate, not a frozen model or
training recipe. A generic DINO-margin head must not be fitted merely to rescue
the completed results. Candidate quality under the localization criterion and
semantic ball identity are different targets; a geometric miss is not a new
semantic negative label.

The bounded next action is capture/data preparation because the saved strong
training pool does not establish adequate rejection coverage. Do not promise a
particular classifier, architecture or fit duration before the candidate census
and scene coverage are known. A useful capture can support candidate-centered
recognition or a separate presence approach; it does not authorize either fit.

## Observed training-support gap

The 3,329 original training frames retain 2,941 eligible positives, 147
certain-hidden and 241 masked rows. The saved native pool contains 3,049 candidates
on eligible frames: 2,953 within ten pixels and 96 outside. Seventy of the latter
are farther than ten pixels plus the recorded placement uncertainty: 57 CHI and
13 SAC. That diagnostic distance is not a semantic other-object label or a
formal uncertainty bound. NFHS has one remaining outside-ten candidate. The
masked frames have 111 candidates, all still unscored for training purposes.

Only two native candidates exist across all 147 certain-hidden training frames,
both in CHI. The frames themselves have not disappeared; the narrow strong
candidate set poorly represents the false responses a future candidate scorer
must reject. Current near-perfect fitting also means the old strong pool is
not a diverse sample of the development failures. Do not fit on HOU to repair it.

The earlier training-fit pass retained observations/hashes, but intentionally
discarded raw means/logits. This is why a map capture may require new inference.
It is not a rerun merely to resume or rescore the completed assessment. It retains
previously missing information; the old outcome and loss results stay intact.
Inventory exact saved-map reuse before new calls. Any import must match source,
clock, PNG, actual mean hash and native components; donor context differences
remain explicit and must not alter the canonical training inputs/outputs.

## Fixed capture scope and safeguards

- Same cosine-nine checkpoint, float32, native cached 768x432 triplets, ascending
  float32 contributor means, original source timing and original training blocks.
  No model-tensor updates or alternate checkpoint selection.
- No fitting or HOU/DET/TOR/curated evaluation data. Already permitted SAC
  saved outputs may supply only exact canonical training-frame imports. The original NFHS training
  replay remains secondary and in its historical role. Masked rows may be
  propagated as original input context but receive no new supervision.
- Verify accepted-reference/package/source-role bindings and every training
  cache before execution; restrict the frame store to training blocks before
  reading its image arrays. Never regenerate missing caches silently.
- At most 3,277 chronological triplet forwards, reduced by any exact verified
  saved-map imports. The prepared inventory fixes the actual required subset.
  Retain attempted/returned calls, failures, raw float32 means, pool provenance
  and exact original observation parity. Stop on mismatch without replacing
  original records. No automatic retry or checkpoint fallback.
- Candidate extraction uses the already implemented native plus >.01 pool,
  original two-pixel merge and no cap. Seal extraction before any label-assisted
  coverage diagnosis. Preserve raw maps for later justified alternatives.
  This pool is not enough to cover the old below-.01 gains or DET's full gap;
  no claim otherwise is made, and their stress evidence remains unchanged.
- No selector or new coordinate is published, no new label is authored, and no
  fit follows automatically. The capture's candidate availability is not accuracy.
- Store approximately 4.42 GB of raw means, plus receipts/pools; check actual
  free space and retained-attempt output size. Memory-map arrays and stream
  blocks so map size is not resident RAM. Use existing owned-process GPU
  supervision, telemetry/thermal stops and graphics-app isolation checks for an
  approved GPU execution. Do not change drivers/settings or infer crash repair.

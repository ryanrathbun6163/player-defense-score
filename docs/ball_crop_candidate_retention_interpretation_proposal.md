## Interpretation limits

The memo must keep the original question narrow: whether removing the existing
twenty-candidate cap helped the unchanged four path scorers at the .01 saved
export floor, with associated errors, missingness and resource costs. This does
not establish the effects of changing confidence and cap simultaneously.

Preserve the .20/.35 structural-equivalence references as reused cap20 evidence,
not newly executed uncapped trials. Do not treat candidate burden as
single-output tracker accuracy, a larger heuristic path score as greater
accuracy, or a localization match as a certified ball identity. The selected
diagnostic cases are not the evaluation denominator. Repeated frames, radii,
thresholds and crop hypotheses are not independent samples. Five hidden frames
from one episode cannot establish robust hidden-ball performance.

The summary may identify unresolved questions and describe a possible next
research question. It may not choose a preferred tracker or operating point,
set a quality cutoff, authorize another experiment, claim generalization, or
turn inspected development examples into a training/evaluation split. No player
identity, calibration, box, center, annotation or confidence value changes.

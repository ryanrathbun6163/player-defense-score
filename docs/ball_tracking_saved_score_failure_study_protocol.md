# Saved-score failure study: source and support diagnosis

October 29, 2026. Design and metadata preparation only. The four-fit pilot,
original consumers, independent score audits and 7,468-row posthoc rank/zero
cross-tab are complete and reused. This document specifies their bounded
follow-up; no new scientific endpoint has been measured in this preparation.

The question is: **which recorded source, candidate and training-support
conditions accompany useful points being rejected, wrong ordering, missing
candidates and hidden-frame survivors—and how do successful cases compare?**
The purpose is to choose the next question before changing training or tracking.

## New measurements, fixed before execution

The old reports already contain per-game/block/origin/visibility/target/uncertainty
tables and rank×zero counts. Reuse those results as reconciliation controls.
The new work is the joint source/support explanation below, retaining successful
comparison cases in every applicable denominator.

**A. Failure-state versus source/support tables.** Start with all existing
context-frame states. Cross their original rank and any-match-positive states
with native selected outcome/retention, weak matching support, and full-pool
hidden survival. Distinguish no matching pool candidate, an empty pool, missing
native choice, no positive match, and noneligible reference. A lost native point
can have another matching candidate above zero; report both per-point loss and
any-match availability. Retain rank ties and exact-zero ties separately.

For each game and block, cross these states with the following existing metadata,
one family at a time: accepted visibility and identity certainty; each difficulty
tag and an explicit no-tag category; recorded uncertainty radius and geometry
source; native/weak origin and original extraction/threshold; crop padding;
source `selection_category`; candidate count and matching-candidate count.
Retain `observed_source_content` verbatim without deriving scene classes from
prose. Difficulty tags overlap; their
tables are not a partition. Include unknown/inapplicable categories explicitly.
Do not create a full Cartesian product of every field or select favorable cells.

Frame-only metadata tables have one row per context-frame. Origin, extraction,
support and padding tables instead use candidate-context rows carrying their
parent's full-pool frame state, with candidate counts and distinct-frame counts
reported separately. A frame may occur in multiple candidate strata; those
frame counts are not additive. Do not recompute ranks within origin strata.

**B. Candidate support and paired fitted/held behavior.** For all 18,813 NBA
candidates / 3,179 NBA frames, retain exact fitted and held scores side by side with evaluation
category, fitting target, selected-native status and metadata. Report all sign
transitions (including zero) and fitted→held rank-state transitions on identical
frame IDs. NFHS has no held fit: keep its 819 candidates / 150 frames as secondary
single-score evidence with held values inapplicable. Define score difference as
held minus fitted. Define full-pool rank margin as maximum matching logit minus
maximum eligible-miss logit; positive means match-first, zero means a top tie.
Margin is null with a named reason for no match, no miss or noneligible reference;
do not replace missing maxima with zero or infinity. The original rank state
still applies to a match-only pool. Summarize score differences and rank margins descriptively by game,
origin and target, with support summaries for both retained and rejected groups.
These are differences between distinct fitted models, not calibrated scales or
an isolated intervention. For HOU, use the one existing all-NBA score only.

Original support fields include `producer_candidate.extraction`, `threshold`,
`native_score`, `component_pixels`, `in_source_bounds` and equivalent-extraction
provenance. `native_score` is response-component mass, not automatically peak.
`component_pixels` is response support, not physical ball diameter. Summarize
these quantities only within compatible producer/extraction/threshold strata.
Keep absent peak/shape features absent; no map reconstruction or new feature
extraction is included. Candidate density is an opportunity/confounding variable.

**C. Training-support correspondence.** Attach each fit's actual game/block/frame,
target and origin support, fitting membership and objective weights to the
applicable diagnostic strata. Report candidate counts, unique frames, unique
blocks/games and summed objective mass separately. Preserve all positive,
distant-miss, certain-hidden and fitting-unknown strata, including cells with no
training examples. HOU has no fitting membership. For held contexts, use that
fold's included games, not all-NBA support. This is metadata coverage, not image
similarity or proof that a failure was caused by insufficient training.

The cross-source support key is `(fitting_target_group, native_or_weak)` plus
exactly one of: accepted visibility, one difficulty tag/no-tag, exact uncertainty
radius, extraction+threshold, or padding state. Compute each family separately,
aggregating only that fit's included games. Game/block identity is reported as
support counts/provenance, never required to match the held game/block. Unknown
fitting targets remain an excluded-support category with no objective mass.
HOU uses existing evaluation targets plus its accepted radius to derive this
reporting-only group with the original positive/hidden/distant/boundary rules;
retain original targets and record the derivation. No target is changed or used
for a fit. Historical semantic rows are inapplicable to geometric support keys.
List zero-count groups and unavailable metadata explicitly. These fixed marginal

Numeric metadata remain continuous. Within the declared categorical cells,

Keep the full rows available. No outcome-selected cutpoints, learned bins,

## Interpretation and stopping point

| Observed pattern | Supported next question | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful ordering preserved while points fall below zero | Score comparability/rejection or separate presence evidence | Does not prove calibration or a safe cutoff |
| Ordering failures associated with game/support regime | Representation, context, regularization or coverage | Fitting composition and amount are confounded |
| No matching candidate | Upstream proposal support | Reranking cannot supply an absent point |
| Certain-hidden candidates survive | Explicit absence/visibility evidence | Negative old selected points do not clear other pool survivors |
| Sparse, conflicting or uninformative associations | Report the causal question unresolved | Do not force a training recipe from a diagnostic table |

The weighted geometric objective's .5/.25/.25 mass is not the population prior.
Zero remains the fixed diagnostic reference (>0 survives; =0 is a tie), not a
calibrated probability boundary or adopted cutoff. Adjacent candidates/frames and
overlapping folds are correlated. Cosine nine already saw CHI/DEN/SAC/NFHS;
scorer-held games do not constitute an end-to-end unseen-game test. The current
outcomes were exposed before this protocol; freezing its new endpoints is not
pristine preregistration or an independent validation set.

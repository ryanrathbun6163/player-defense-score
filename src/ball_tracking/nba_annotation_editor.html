Annotation rules

A point must be defensible in this frame. A partial ball uses an estimate of the whole ball’s center, not the visible fragment. Fully hidden or indeterminate frames have no point. Uncertainty is a source-pixel radius, not confidence. Exclusion requires a protocol/source reason; difficulty alone is not a reason to exclude. Saving labels never marks human review complete.
